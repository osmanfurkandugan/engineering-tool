ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for Practical Engineering

Electrical Tool is a growing collection of practical calculators and tools designed for electrical engineers, technicians, students, and anyone working with electrical systems.

Our goal is simple: to make engineering calculations faster, clearer, and more accessible.

From voltage drop calculations to cable sizing, power systems, transformers, and other electrical engineering applications, Electrical Tool aims to provide reliable and easy-to-use tools for everyday engineering work.

We believe that engineering tools should be practical, intuitive, and accessible. That is why we focus on creating simple interfaces that help users get the information they need without unnecessary complexity.

Electrical Tool is continuously evolving, with new calculators and features being added over time.

Whether you are a professional engineer checking a design, a technician working on an electrical installation, or a student learning the fundamentals of electrical engineering, we hope Electrical Tool can make your work a little easier.

Your feedback matters to us. If you have suggestions for new calculators, ideas for improving existing tools, or notice an error, we would love to hear from you. Your feedback helps us improve Electrical Tool and build tools that are genuinely useful for the engineering community.
