Electrical Engineering Calculators

Electrical Tool is built by an electrical engineer, with the help of AI, to give students and enthusiasts free access to core electrical engineering calculators. The goal is to grow this into a community project — shaped by user feedback, with new tools and improvements added based on what people here actually need.
